/ About

I grew up in Nepal in a family where money was always short but curiosity never was. I taught myself quantum computing on borrowed library machines, worked on real IBM Quantum hardware at nineteen, and published my first peer-reviewed paper before leaving Kathmandu.

I’m now a Presidential Premier Honors Scholar at FIU with a 3.86 GPA, majoring in Mathematical Data Science. Research takes up most of my week: neural-network pruning with Dr. Bernard Gerstman in the Physics Department, and a conductance-based model of the Drosophila Giant Fiber System with the Pena Lab (FAU / FIU / FSU collaboration).

The rest of my time goes to teaching. I’m a Learning Assistant for Calculus and Physics, an FIUteach fellow at South Miami Middle School, and a peer mentor for incoming international students. If I know something, I try to pass it along.
