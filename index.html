Quantum · Research

Variational quantum eigensolver

Quantum ML Research Assistant · Data Management Research Lab, FIU · with Prof. Janki Bhimani · May 2026 – Present

A VQE tunes a parameterized circuit to find a molecule’s ground-state energy; the optimizer walks down an energy landscape like the one on the left. How big each step should be is the research question.

  • Hyperparameter-free optimizers: a two-phase adaptive step size that bounds suboptimality through energy variance (Weinstein and Temple inequalities).
  • Proved the schedule equivalent to normalized imaginary-time evolution under Fubini–Study geometry.
